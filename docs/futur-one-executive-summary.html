Build economics — cost per MW falls with scale 01

Construction cost per megawatt declines as the campus grows: fixed site costs (substation, land, soft costs, control room) dilute across more MW while procurement moves to bulk. ~80% of the scale benefit is captured by 150 MW; beyond that the linear MEP/cooling load dominates and the curve flattens to the ~$7.8M floor.
