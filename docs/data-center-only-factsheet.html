Investment Thesis 01

  • Pure infrastructure, zero technology risk. Hearst is the landlord of powered capacity — the client brings its own compute. Returns track real estate, not chip cycles.
  • Contracted, escalating cash flow. 15-year hyperscale leases at $175/kW/month, 3% annual escalation, 90% stabilized occupancy. Hard-currency, investment-grade counterparties.
  • Structural cost advantage. Qatar industrial power at ~$32/MWh — among the lowest globally — and 0% corporate tax under the QFZA Free Zone regime (20-year holiday).
  • Sovereign demand anchor. Google, Microsoft and Oracle cloud regions are already live in Doha (25–80 MW each). National AI strategy underwrites multi-decade demand.
  • Bankable exit. Stabilized Tier III colocation trades at 15–18× EBITDA; a 16× exit in 2035 yields a $1.57B terminal value.
