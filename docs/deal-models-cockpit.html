01

The 6 deal models used across the industry

From the least capital-intensive for the operator (lease) to the most committing (equity JV). All coexist.

A · capital-light for the operator
Equity Joint-Venture (JV)
The classic Equinix / Digital Realty play

The operator pairs with long-term capital (sovereign fund, pension, PE) in a shared vehicle that owns the assets. The operator keeps a minority (20–25%) but retains development + operations for recurring fees.

Upside
+ Moves debt off balance sheet, keeps the ops
+ Recurring asset/dev management fees
+ Scales capacity without diluting own equity
Trade-off
– Shares the upside (capital takes majority)
– Shared governance on big calls
B · the user dictates the spec
Build-to-Suit (BTS)
Developer builds for a single tenant

A developer finances and builds to a hyperscaler’s exact spec under a long lease (often 15y+). An investment-grade tenant’s lease ≈ near-bond paper — highly bankable.

Upside
+ Revenue secured from delivery (pre-leased)
+ Long lease = easy financing, predictable IRR
Trade-off
– Single-tenant = concentration risk
– Tenant captures the flexibility, not the landlord
C · the lease is the product
Wholesale lease / Powered shell
Leased in MW, 5–15 year terms

The operator delivers the powered shell + cooling + connectivity; the tenant racks its own servers. Priced per MW. The tenant’s credit is the real underlying — a Microsoft/AWS lease is close to investment-grade.

Upside
+ Triple-net: tenant carries tax, insurance, maintenance
+ Ticket 1–20 MW per suite/hall
Trade-off
– Margin per unit lower than retail colo
– Value capped by lease terms
D · a fund replaces the partner
Fund / development vehicle
Digital Realty Hyperscale Fund ($3.25B)

The operator raises a dedicated fund from institutional investors, keeps ~20% and operates everything. The industrialized variant of the JV: one vehicle, many LPs, a pipeline of assets.

Upside
+ Scalable: reload the vehicle each vintage
+ Operator stays the sole GP / operator
Trade-off
– Needs a credible pipeline to attract LPs
– GP economics, not full ownership
E · AI changes the game
Neocloud / GPU off-take
CoreWeave, Nscale, Nebius, Fluidstack…

Born of AI: a compute buyer (hyperscaler or AI lab) signs a massive multi-year off-take contract that collateralizes the build + GPU purchase. The contract IS the financing.

Upside
+ Microsoft: $60B+ to Nscale, Nebius, CoreWeave, Iren, Lambda
+ Anthropic ↔ Fluidstack: ~$50B of Claude-dedicated DC
+ Levered IRR 25–35%+ with GPU residual
Trade-off
– GPU obsolescence / residual-value exposure
– Counterparty concentration on one buyer
F · the state as co-owner
Sovereign / national-champion JV
The Gulf model — G42, HUMAIN, Qai

The state (via a national entity) takes the controlling majority to keep sovereignty; tech players bring tech, GPUs and skills in minority. The mirror image of model A.

Upside
+ Stargate UAE: G42 60% / OpenAI·Oracle·NVIDIA·SoftBank 40%
+ Data + governance stay in-country
Trade-off
– Global partner gives up control
– Politically gated, slower to close

Real deals — what the majors have signed

The recurring structure: majority long-term capital + minority operator that keeps operational control.

Equinix × GIC × CPP — xScale US> $15B
Equity JV · 2024

GIC 37.5% · CPP Investments 37.5% · Equinix 25%. Equity + debt for >$15B. Target: 100MW+ campuses, >1.5GW of US hyperscale capacity. Equinix develops and operates for fees.

Equinix × GIC — Europe / Japan / Korea$0.5 → 3.9B
Equity JV · 2019–2022

First xScale vehicles: GIC 80% · Equinix 20%. Replicated region by region (Europe, Japan, Korea, APAC), then topped up +$3.9B. Equinix keeps 20% and the operations.

Digital Realty × Blackstone$7B
Development JV · 2024

Blackstone 80% (~$700M initial equity) · Digital Realty 20%. 4 campuses across Frankfurt, Paris & Northern Virginia (3 metros), ~500 MW. Digital Realty runs dev + operations, earns fees.

Digital Realty × TPGNoVA portfolio
Stabilized JV · 2023

TPG Real Estate takes the majority of 3 stabilized DCs (Northern Virginia); Digital Realty keeps a minority + operates. The "already-leased assets" variant: monetize stabilized, not development.

Stargate UAE — G42 + OpenAI/Oracle/NVIDIA/SoftBankup to $10B
Sovereign JV · May 2025

G42 60% (national control) · tech consortium 40%. Target: 1 GW of AI compute in Abu Dhabi. Sovereignty mandates local control — the inverse of the Equinix model.

Microsoft × G42 — UAE$15.2B
Equity + capacity · 2024–29

Microsoft takes a stake in G42 and co-expands capacity (+200 MW). $15.2B total country commitment (incl. $7.9B new for 2026–2029). Mixes equity participation and capacity deployment.

MEEZA × hyperscaler — Qatar750M QAR
Wholesale lease · 2025

~$206M, 6 MW phase 1 → 44 MW over time. Wholesale-lease model, no shared equity. The bankable, revenue-first path Qatar has actually signed.

Ooredoo × Oracle — Qatarplatform licence
Sovereign cloud · 2025

Deploys Oracle Alloy: sovereign cloud + AI delivered from Qatar. Platform-licence model — data and governance stay local. Sovereignty via software, not equity.

04

Who does what in a hyperscale deal

A deal can involve up to 5 distinct roles. One actor can hold several — but thinking in roles tells you what you bring and what you capture.

The long-term capital
GIC, CPP, Blackstone, TPG, sovereign funds

Brings the majority equity. Wants stable returns backed by investment-grade leases. Does not want to operate.

The operator / developer
Equinix, Digital Realty, Vantage, NTT

Brings dev + operations know-how, client relationships, pipeline. Keeps 20–25% + fees. The "engine" of the deal.

The capacity taker
Microsoft, AWS, Google, Meta, Oracle, AI labs

The tenant / compute buyer. Its credit drives bankability. In AI, its off-take contract IS the financing.

The tech supplier
NVIDIA (GPU), Oracle/OpenAI (cloud/models)

Brings silicon, platform, models. Often minority in equity but central to the AI value chain.

Land & power
Land, grid connection, power (gas, solar, grid)

The rare, decisive underlying: no available MW, no deal. A holder of land + power has real negotiating leverage.

The state / sovereign
Gulf model — national entity

In the Gulf, the state enters the cap table in the majority to keep data + governance. Inverts the usual balance of power.

05

Gulf / Qatar specifics

The regional pattern differs from the West: sovereignty first, the state/national champion owns, tech enters in minority.

The sovereign rule

UAE (G42), Saudi (HUMAIN), Qatar (Qai) are national champions coordinating capital, energy and compute. On Stargate UAE, G42 keeps 60% so sovereign AI infra stays under national control. Data does not leave the country — hence the sovereign-cloud wave (Oracle Alloy at Ooredoo).

What Qatar has already signed

MEEZA × hyperscaler — 750M QAR (~$206M), 6 MW phase 1 → 44 MW at term. Wholesale-lease model, no shared equity.

Ooredoo × Oracle — deploys Oracle Alloy: sovereign cloud + AI delivered from Qatar. Platform-licence model, data stays local.
