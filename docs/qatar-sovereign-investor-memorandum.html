Investment Memorandum — 03 / 06
The Power-to-Cash Corridor

Qatar can compress the path from power to contracted cash

03

Value comes from control of power, not construction alone. With sovereign coordination, power allocation, land, permitting and anchor demand can be aligned earlier than a merchant greenfield — compressing the critical path. Capacity is targeted to be contracted ahead of energization; each tranche activates cash on delivery, toward a $405M stabilized run-rate within ~18 months of first power.

Conditions Precedent & Methodology
  • Headline economics represent the Core Contracted Case and are illustrative — not a forecast, valuation, or guarantee of returns.
  • Subject to execution of anchor lease(s) with creditworthy counterparties on the modelled terms and duration.
  • Subject to a dedicated power allocation / PPA (QatarEnergy / Kahramaa) sufficient for the 150 MW load.
  • Subject to site control, permitting, engineering, financing and full counterparty due diligence.
