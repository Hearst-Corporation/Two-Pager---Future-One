## 🎵 MUSIC PROMPT (for Suno / Udio / MusicGen)

### Single track, 2:40, 4 sections

```
Cinematic orchestral hybrid score, 2 minutes 40 seconds.

Section 1 (0:00–0:15) — TENSION
Sub-bass drone in C minor, distant low brass swells, single sustained
synth pad, build-up with rising white noise, no percussion.

Section 2 (0:15–1:00) — INDUSTRIAL DRIVE
110 BPM. Pulsing electronic kick on every beat. Layered analog synth
arpeggios in 16th notes. Distorted bass guitar. Industrial percussion
hits. Tension rising. Cinematic Hans Zimmer × Daft Punk × Blade Runner.

Section 3 (1:00–1:10) — DROP / BREATH
Sudden silence. Single piano note. Distant choir hum. Reverb tail.

Section 4 (1:10–1:40) — WARM AWAKENING
90 BPM. Soft piano arpeggios in C major. Warm string section enters
gently. Subtle vinyl crackle. Hopeful, expansive. Cinematic Ludovico
Einaudi × Max Richter.

Section 5 (1:40–2:15) — JOY RISING
Same key, building intensity. Add strings ensemble (full orchestra),
gentle female choir "ahh", subtle hand claps, light percussion. Warm,
joyful, alive, golden hour vibe. Cinematic Hans Zimmer "Time" × A.R. Rahman.

Section 6 (2:15–2:40) — TRANSCENDENT CLOSING
Climax with full orchestra, piano lead melody, single breath of choir,
then slow decay to silence with sub-bass pulse. Final piano note rings out.
```
