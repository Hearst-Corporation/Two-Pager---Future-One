The Opportunity

Why Now
Global AI compute demand is outstripping supply, and power is the binding constraint. Qatar holds among the world’s lowest marginal cost of dispatchable power — a structural moat for the next compute decade.
Market Context
Hyperscaler & sovereign AI capex exceeds US$400B / year. GPU supply is normalizing while power-ready land is scarce; sub-$0.03/kWh power is a durable advantage.
Strategic Rationale
Monetize stranded gas into 24/7 compute. Sovereign diversification beyond LNG. Anchor Qatar as the MENA AI hub with first-mover scale.
