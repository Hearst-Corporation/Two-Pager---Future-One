02The Opportunity

Power is the binding constraint.
Qatar holds the structural advantage.

Why Now

Global AI compute demand is outstripping supply, and power has become the binding constraint. Qatar holds among the world’s lowest marginal cost of dispatchable power — the scarce input the entire sector is competing for.

Market Context

Hyperscaler and sovereign AI capex exceeds US$400B per year. GPU supply is normalizing while power-ready land remains scarce; sub-US$0.03/kWh power is a structural moat that cannot be quickly replicated elsewhere.

Strategic Rationale

Monetize stranded gas into 24/7 compute and diversify the sovereign economy beyond LNG. Futur One anchors Qatar as the MENA AI hub and converts an energy advantage into a durable digital-export franchise.

The Thesis

“AI is the new gas.” The highest-value export of the next decade is not the molecule — it is the intelligence it powers. Futur One captures the spread at the source.

Key Value Drivers
i.
US$0.028/kWh all-in power cost — among the lowest on earth.
ii.
North Field expansion CCGT supplying dedicated 24/7 generation.
iii.
Anchor hyperscaler 10-year take-or-pay underpinning revenue.
iv.
Sovereign co-investment and ECA financing lowering cost of capital.
