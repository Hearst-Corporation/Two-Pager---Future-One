Why one company is worth more

Raw datacenter compute trades at a low multiple (~6× revenue, capital-intensive). Wrapped inside a B2B model-training business, the same capacity earns a services multiple (~15×) — re-rating the whole company.

The thesis in one line. The datacenter supplies the compute; the B2B training business sells outcomes on top of it — fine-tuned, deployed, domain-specific models on open foundations (Llama 4, DeepSeek, Qwen 3, Kimi K2.5, MiniMax M2.1). That earns a services multiple (~15×) rather than an infrastructure one (~6×), so every $1 of training ARR adds far more value than $1 of raw compute — lifting the single company well above the sum of its hardware.
